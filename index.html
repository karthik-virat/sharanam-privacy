Privacy Policy

Sharanam - Ayyappa Pooja (శరణం - అయ్యప్ప పూజ) · Last updated 14 November 2026

In short

Sharanam - Ayyappa Pooja is a free devotional app for Sri Ayyappa Swamy pooja, made by Velamala Karthik. It works offline and does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal information. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Information stored on your phone only

To work, the app keeps a few settings on your device:

  • your name and gotra (for the sankalpam), and the place you choose;
  • your mandala deeksha start date and length;
  • your japa count, where you stopped in the pooja, and display choices (language, theme, text size, speed);
  • links to audio/video files you add from your phone (if any).

None of this leaves your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Location (optional)

Only if you tap “Use my current location”, the app reads your approximate location once to find your place and calculate sunrise, tithi and the sankalpam details. It is used only on your phone and is never sent anywhere. You can instead pick your place from a list.

Internet

The pooja text and recorded chants are built into the app and work without internet. The app goes online only if you set a media list URL in Settings or play/download an online audio or video item you added; those requests go to the server hosting that content, which may log your IP address under its own policy.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state – only for the optional online media above.
  • Approximate location – optional, as described above.
  • Vibrate – feedback on the japa counter.
  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback) – playback controls while chants play.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files (except files you choose to add).

Voluntary support

Some versions of the app offer an optional way to support it via your own UPI app. The payment happens entirely in your UPI app; this app never sees, receives or stores any payment details. The Google Play version does not include this option.

Children

The app is a general devotional reference. It contains no ads, accounts, chat or tracking, and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
